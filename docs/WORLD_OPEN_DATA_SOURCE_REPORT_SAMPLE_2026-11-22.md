# ENIGMA sample intelligence report — Which worldwide open-data sources can support commercial research?
**Research date:** 22 November 2026, Africa/Kampala  
**Status:** Real source/licensing desk research; **NOT** a commissioned report, not proof a worker outside this session executed, and not proof of buyer or paid receipt.

## Executive question and buyer value
A business owner wants a source-backed initial shortlist of global public datasets that a research team can analyze for country, market, competitor, mapping or industry work **without assuming all publicly readable information is commercially reusable**. Our original service is selection, checking, joining and interpreting the permitted data, with methods and dates—not ownership of raw public datasets.

**Method:** Read the listed primary provider license/access pages and classify source scope; no mass dataset retrieval was performed. The report does not imply that any 2026 indicator series, actual entity count, commodity price or individual tender has been fetched.

## Findings
1. **License screening is a billable research step, not trivial overhead.** World's data catalogs mix open, attribution, special-license and potentially non-commercial records; especially check a dataset's publisher and exact terms before a paid report.
2. **Structured data and metadata are not the same as commercial conclusions.** Wikidata can support a rough candidate list and Data.gov a source index; both require verification and an original, customer-specific question.
3. **Global comparability is limited without definitions.** Before comparing countries or years, record indicator definition, reporting period, units, whether missing values were imputed, and source changes.
4. **The real deliverable is a well-defined decision.** A professional report identifies high-confidence findings, gaps and recommended actions; a dump of public URLs has little defensible value.
