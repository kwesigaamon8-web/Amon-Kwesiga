**Original comparative calculations from the published rounded figures:**
- Highest annual consumer-price inflation among these three in 2025: Kenya (4.1%). **Range: 0.8 percentage points** (4.1 minus 3.3).
- Highest remittances as a proportion of GDP: Uganda (4.5%), compared with Kenya (3.8%) and Tanzania (1.4%). The figures **do not measure absolute USD remittance totals**.
- Uganda's published FDI net-inflow **GDP ratio** (5.4%) exceeds Kenya's ratio (0.8%) by **4.6 percentage points**; these are not monetary investment amounts or future investment forecasts.
