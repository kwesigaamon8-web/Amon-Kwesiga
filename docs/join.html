How people join

1. Open our GitHub collaborator form. Share your skill and a public/synthetic sample. 2. Choose one bounded task. 3. Agree scope, review rights, and if applicable compensation or mining payout splits **before** work. 4. Deliver a testable result; record actual acceptance. One form submission is an inquiry—not a hired worker or a verified ASIC.

Independent collaborators, transparent financial terms

We do not promise salaries, referral bonuses, coin deposits, pool shares, employment, guaranteed customers, or passive income. Existing B1/B2 worker roles are program definitions, not evidence of a real person or an executing AI. No recruiting friends into a fake earning scheme. Any real paid project needs named scope, buyer acceptance, amount, payment asset/network, and a separately agreed split.

Never paste seed phrases, passwords, bank records, other people's personal information, private datasets, or wallet keys into a public issue. Mining must use only hardware the operator owns or is expressly authorized to control. We do not mine on free hosting or other people's devices, and no miner's rewards are sent to the project's wallet without clear advance consent.

A lightweight scheduled GitHub job checks public opt-in messages periodically; it does not maintain a staffed 24/7 chat room, send DMs or automatically accept applicants. Publication does not establish recruitment, an employment relationship, a pool partnership or crypto earnings.
