**Subject:** Screening public digital-work marketplaces before bidding

## Decision question
Which public leads deserve further qualification if a small software/data team wants legitimate paid work without paying to enter or giving third parties wallet-signing authority?

## Evidence-led qualification method
1. Open the **original issuer's** listing. Record its exact URL, publication time, scope, deadline, reward amount and eligibility.
2. Confirm credible proof of funds/escrow where applicable. A leaderboard or advertised USD prize alone is **not** verified funds.
3. Check the submission route, account permission, any fees, exclusion policies and whether BTC mainnet or native Circle USDC on Base can be paid to an **approved receive-only address**.
4. Estimate effort, acceptance test and competition; reject unsigned-wallet demands, required deposits or unknown chains without owner approval.
5. Contact the actual task issuer only in a permitted reply/submission channel with one relevant offer; log the reply, acceptance and eventual verified on-chain receipt separately.

## Verdict and uncertainty
**Candidate research routes, not sales opportunities qualified for immediate execution.** None of the three sources above proves that an ENIGMA worker has been hired or paid. Each current individual bounty requires a separate check with the originating issuer before work.
