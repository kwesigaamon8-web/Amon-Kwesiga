Business support available

1. Monthly URA and payroll compliance

Prepare or review VAT, PAYE, withholding tax and other applicable returns; reconcile URA ledger balances; review EFRIS invoice consistency; summarize actions requiring approval.

2. Bookkeeping and bank reconciliation

Post and review accounting entries; trace bank movements to supporting records; reconcile cashbook, bank, customer and supplier balances; deliver an exceptions list for unresolved differences.

3. Financial statements and management reports

Prepare schedules, profit and loss, balance sheet and management reporting from the client's actual records, with documented assumptions and matters to resolve.

4. EFRIS and audit preparation

Compare EFRIS invoices with books, flag duplicates or missing records, organize the supporting documents and prepare audit-ready reconciliations. Statutory audit opinions require a separately engaged authorized auditor.
