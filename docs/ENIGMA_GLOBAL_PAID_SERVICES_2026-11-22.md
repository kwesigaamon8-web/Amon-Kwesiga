## Custom services: available for scoped quotes

We can propose, build, independently test and human-review **new** solutions after a buyer agrees an achievable scope. Categories are capabilities to quote, not claims that every possible finished product already exists.

### Public-source intelligence (OSINT): lawfully collected, attributable sources only

- **Public organization and company profiles**: official registry extracts where accessible, published leadership and corporate announcements, domain and website observations, public products, dated citations and clear uncertainties.
- **Competitor and pricing landscape**: publicly advertised competing services and dated price comparisons; no account bypassing or scraped private customer data.
- **Market and sector intelligence**: geography, demand signals, public market data, regulation summaries with official links; clear methods and dated sources.
- **Public tender, grant, research-call and bounty monitoring**: genuine original issuers, deadlines, eligibility, budgets and submission rules; no fake funded opportunities.
- **Public website, product and source-code due diligence**: live-page availability checks, public repository metadata, published licensing and technical quality observations.
- **Public supply chain / supplier landscape**: businesses that publicly identify themselves as sellers, their public offerings and verification leads; no disclosure of private contact lists.
- **Research briefs and evidence tables**: a sourced spreadsheet, annotated link list, transparent uncertainty, date of retrieval and summary of actionable findings.
- **Public cryptocurrency transaction evidence**: accurately report observable transactions and contract/network details; a public address does not establish wallet ownership or payer identity.
- **Public geospatial and infrastructure summaries**: published maps/open datasets and reproducible citations, without exposing sensitive personal locations or critical vulnerabilities.

**OSINT restrictions:** No personal stalking, doxxing, private identity profiling, stolen/credential-gated datasets, private account access, de-anonymization, intrusive surveillance, illicit-market sourcing, hacking or claims that publicly available data proves a person's guilt. Every report distinguishes observed evidence, inference and unknowns. Open-source means legally/publicly accessible and properly attributed—not unrestricted reuse.
