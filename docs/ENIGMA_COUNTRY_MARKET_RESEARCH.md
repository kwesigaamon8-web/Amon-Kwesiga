Uganda trial software: learning attendance, rainwater estimates, fresh produce waste and electricity budget.

## 775-billion virtual role dispatch

`crypto-agents/marketing/country_market_research.py --all` creates 249 bounded country/territory research briefs. `--global-index N` assigns one brief to a virtual worker ID without starting billions of live processes. B1/B2 role messages carry those assignments to actual executing worker processes only if such processes exist and run.

Each deliverable must record: jurisdiction, date and source, one local problem with evidence, app link SW-001 through SW-1000, language and accessibility fit, legal/licensing checks, permitted distribution channel, direct post URL if published, real customer inquiry, accepted work, and independently verified native-chain cryptocurrency receipt if any.

**The country work is not complete simply because role instructions exist.** No unsolicited email, private contact scraping, fake accounts, bots masquerading as users, spam or automatic mining on visitor hardware.
