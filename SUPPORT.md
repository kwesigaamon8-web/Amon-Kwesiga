# Support ENIGMA's free, original software

ENIGMA / B1–B2 Digital Work Studio makes original browser utilities, reproducible examples and small applications freely available. **Use of the software does not require a donation, login, wallet connection or payment.** Contributions are completely voluntary and do not purchase work, guarantee future access or establish a debt.

There is no minimum amount, recurring charge or automatic wallet action.

**This is independent software-project support, not a charity appeal, tax-deductible charitable gift, investment, staking arrangement, token sale or promise of a financial return.** No donation is owed or required, and no received amount is claimed by publishing this page.

## Want to buy a specific digital service instead?

That is a **different transaction**: [inspect five original, fixed-scope examples and buyer terms](./docs/agent-work-market.html), then [submit a non-sensitive quote request](https://github.com/kwesigaamon8-web/Amon-Kwesiga/issues/new?template=paid-work-request.yml), or [ask publicly without a GitHub account](https://www.task-bounty.com/board/43a7a645-082f-4f5f-87b8-f5397ec9c121) if the third-party board is working. Before a paid job begins, buyer and operator must agree scope, accepted outputs, exact fee, recipient and payment network. The B1 native Base-USDC route is **conditional on an individually agreed paid job**, not a general donation address.

### Evidence and privacy

The published wallets are not evidence of ownership, a donation, a paid invoice or a confirmed transaction. ENIGMA records earned revenue only when it can link an accepted service to a real correct-network receipt. No personal client data, tax/audit records, emails or credentials belong in public issues. ENIGMA is separate from the owner's professional accounting and consulting work.
