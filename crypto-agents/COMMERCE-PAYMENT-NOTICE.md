## How an actual payment works

1. A prospective customer sends a task scope, sanitized example and requested currency through the [opt-in intake](https://github.com/kwesigaamon8-web/Amon-Kwesiga/issues/18). A potential supporter can respond through our [B1](https://github.com/kwesigaamon8-web/Amon-Kwesiga/issues/10) or [B2](https://github.com/kwesigaamon8-web/Amon-Kwesiga/issues/11) public discussions.
2. Before payment, the owner and payer agree on **one** destination and **exact** currency, blockchain/network, asset/token contract if applicable, required memo and compatible receiver. No signing, wallet connection, fee-to-unlock, or private keys are ever requested.
3. For a paid service, the authorized workers agree the scope and customer acceptance terms, produce original deliverables, and verify receipt independently. A wallet address, ad, GitHub test, promised contribution or simulated transaction is **not** proof of receipt.
