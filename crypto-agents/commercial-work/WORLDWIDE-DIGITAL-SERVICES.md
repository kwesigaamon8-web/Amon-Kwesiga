### How to commission

1. Open [the paid-service intake issue](https://github.com/kwesigaamon8-web/Amon-Kwesiga/issues/18) or use a legitimate authorized reply to an existing customer conversation.
2. State the **specific outcome** needed and provide a synthetic/anonymized sample, acceptance criteria, planned delivery date and target budget. Never publish private customer files, employee records or credentials in public issues.
3. After scope, payer and authorized workers are confirmed, provide one small sample or a full quote. Only then agree on contracting terms and start paid client work.
4. Deliver the original result and acceptance evidence. Invoice through a mutually approved route. Crypto asset, exact chain, ownership and destination must be confirmed for each real buyer.
