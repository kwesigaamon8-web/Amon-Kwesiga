This is a *portfolio of proposed paid-service experiments*, not a list of contracted customers. Each authorized future run must find NEW evidence rather than recycling a generic idea. **Do not claim a worker has executed unless its real job/model/process produced an auditable output.** The huge logical roster is an addressable hierarchy, not billions of online processes.

## Chief D's mandatory decision loop

1. **Verify demand.** Find one dated primary-source request from a named real buyer, or a genuine reply to an owner-operated listing. Confirm that applications are still allowed, the customer can engage the owner and no upfront charge is necessary. An unclaimed/advertised bounty is not escrow.
2. **Test a concrete deliverable.** Select a useful small output that can be independently checked within the authorized compute and human-review budget. Build a free generic demonstration if helpful, but do not expose buyer data or present a free demo as paid work.
3. **Offer a clear commercial agreement.** Write a permission-based personalized proposal with scope, fee, acceptance test, settlement rails and human-approved commitments. Do not impersonate the owner or submit anything through unauthenticated accounts.
4. **Execute actual paid work after agreement.** Assign a genuine worker with real process evidence, create an original result, test it, deliver it in an approved channel and obtain buyer acknowledgment. If there is no buyer agreement, pursue the next verifiable need.
5. **Verify collection.** Count only independently validated cleared cash or correct-network crypto receipt as collected. Record deliverables and accepted-but-unpaid amounts separately. Avoid incorrectly calling speculative bids or on-chain self-transfers sales.
