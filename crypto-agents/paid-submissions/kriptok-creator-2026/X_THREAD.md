# KriptoK Creator Challenge — original 10-post X thread

**Published:** No. **Submitted:** No. **Reward:** None earned. **Content disclosure:** Research-based educational post using official public KriptoK materials, not a claim of firsthand app use.

1/ Self-custody is not just “my keys, my coins.” It’s also “my checklist.” I looked at @KriptoK’s public docs and put together a 10-point guide to the mistakes I’d want to avoid before moving funds. No trades needed to learn this. 🧵

2/ First question: WHO CONTROLS THE KEYS? KriptoK says keys remain on your device. That means more control—but also more responsibility. Never paste a recovery phrase into a website, chatbot, form or support DM.

3/ Second: WHICH NETWORK? KriptoK’s website lists 13 supported networks. That does NOT mean every token is supported on every route. Confirm the asset, chain and receiving address in the actual app before you send anything.

4/ Third: CAN I RECOVER? A phone can be replaced. A lost recovery phrase may be permanent. Back it up offline, check that you can read it correctly, and never share it. Self-custody without recovery planning is a hidden risk.

5/ Fourth: WHAT DOES THE SWAP DO? KriptoK describes same-chain and cross-chain swaps. A route with several steps is not identical to a direct transfer. Check the destination asset/network and the confirmed quote—not just the button label.

6/ Fifth: IS “EARN” GUARANTEED? No. Onchain vaults can show variable APYs, and smart contracts or underlying assets can fail. Understand who takes the risk, when you can withdraw and what happens if the protocol stops working.

7/ Sixth: AM I BUYING THE ASSET OR A DERIVATIVE? KriptoK lists perps and tokenized stocks. A perpetual position is not ownership of the underlying stock or coin. Leverage adds liquidation risk; a fancy interface cannot remove it.

8/ Seventh: WHAT IS THE SCORE ACTUALLY MEASURING? @KriptoKLeague has a trading leaderboard and published eligibility rules, including capital and filled-order requirements. Read the current rules rather than assuming leaderboard rank equals profit.

9/ Eighth: WHAT WOULD I TEST FIRST? I’d verify the source URL, the selected chain, the app’s current product support, and the wallet history—before considering a small transfer. I have not made a live KriptoK transaction for this thread.

10/ The point: a good self-custody tool should make decisions clearer, not make caution disappear. My test: can a first-time user explain each confirmation before clicking it? What is the ONE wallet check you never skip? @KriptoK #KriptoK
