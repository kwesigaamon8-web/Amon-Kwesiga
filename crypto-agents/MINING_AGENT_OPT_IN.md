## Option A — miner voluntarily directs future earnings from their own hashrate

Public Pool official reference: https://web.public-pool.io/

Public Pool presently documents **different** mining modes:

- **Solo BTC:** `stratum+tcp://public-pool.io:3333` or TLS `stratum+tls://public-pool.io:4333`. A miner earns only if its work finds a Bitcoin block. A miner who chooses this route may use `BTC_ADDRESS.optional_worker_label` as its miner username. This has extreme variance, especially for small hashrate.
- **PPLNS shared rewards:** `stratum+tcp://public-pool.io:13333` or TLS `stratum+tls://public-pool.io:14333`. Pool rewards are split according to the pool's actual share-based payout rules if blocks are found; they are not guaranteed just because the miner submits shares.

Example **SOLO** username for Company 1:
`bc1qvprle87txnk7dgcgsau0nauxvvkwuyp0w9zc0z.voluntaryminer`

Example **PPLNS** username for Company 2:
`bc1qtaunz7mx7k588p8v544tq7x7ezxxcfky0h2vn9.voluntaryminer`

These are examples of a miner's **voluntary** setting; they are not login credentials, active miners or confirmed payouts. Miners should independently inspect the official pool rules and verify their equipment and wallet compatibility before choosing any mode. No pool can promise a Bitcoin block. CPUs and tiny educational miners have negligible odds of creating meaningful BTC revenue; do not purchase hardware or rent hashrate for this programme without a separate cost/benefit analysis.

## Option B — independent agent earns payment for useful work and voluntarily pays

An independent agent or human operator may offer lawful, genuinely completed services such as documentation, data cleanup, source verification, bug fixes or spreadsheet work, with a clear agreement and explicit acceptance before requesting payment. If their customer or operator independently wants to contribute a payment or tip, they can use a company-specific receiving BTC address. The wallet receipt must be checked on Bitcoin mainnet and identified separately as a **paid job**, **voluntary gift**, **self-transfer**, or **unknown origin**.

No unsolicited repeated GitHub comments, artificial claims of employer contracts, requests for access to another agent's account, credential sharing, wallet-draining, malware or unauthorized computing. No claim of earning before a verified on-chain output.

## Chief D evidence checklist

For every miner/agent opportunity: original URL, actual verified contact or consent status, chosen company wallet, mode/asset/network, agreed terms (if any), real authenticated message link, demonstrable equipment/hashrate **if consented and verifiable**, pool status, genuine accepted work if paid, and independently verifiable **txid + output + amount** after receipt. Report `NOT_VERIFIED` unless evidence supports a stronger statement.
