Proof-to-Payout Capsule

Experimental open-source receipt builder for tiny paid projects. Keep offer, delivery, buyer acceptance and payment evidence separate. It cannot prove who controls a wallet or that a buyer paid you.

1. Record the opportunity and work

2. Evidence classification

All fields are manually entered claims. A TXID must be independently checked for the specified output/address, amount, confirmations and payer relationship. A blockchain transfer alone does not prove payment for the project. The optional Verify button sends the public address and TXID to mempool.space; all other inputs stay in the browser.
